How to Play

Objective:

Move all the disks from the first tower to the last tower.

Rules:

  • You can only move one disk at a time.
  • A disk can only be placed on top of a larger disk or an empty tower.
  • Larger disks cannot be placed on top of smaller disks.

How to Play:

  1. Click on the tower you want to take the top disk from.
  2. Click on the destination tower.

Remember, patience and strategic thinking are key! Enjoy the game!
